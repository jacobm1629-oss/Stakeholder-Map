Frontline Information Infrastructure

The operational disconnect between centralized university operations and student-facing intake desks. Frontline staff bear the brunt of confusion with zero live maintenance visibility.

Core Stake / Need: Immediate, closed-loop status visibility for facilities work orders and administrative answers.
Friction / Worries: Deflection loops push unresolved questions onto peer RAs, creating dead ends and learned helplessness.
Institutional Leverage / Power: Controls shift-level SOPs and whether students receive helpful resolutions or silence.
